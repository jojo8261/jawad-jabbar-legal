At Jawad Jabbar (“we,” “our,” or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name, username, and email address.
  • Contact information and communication preferences.
  • Account registration and profile details.
  • Messages, support requests, feedback, and survey responses.
  • Content, documents, images, prompts, or files submitted to our Services.

Payment card information may be processed directly by third-party payment providers. We will describe the relevant arrangements based on the payment methods we actually support.

B. Information collected automatically

When you access our Website or Services, we may collect technical and usage information, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type, operating system, and device information.
  • Pages visited, features used, and interactions with our Services.
  • Date, time, and duration of visits.
  • Referring URLs and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Cookies, device identifiers, and similar technical information.

C. Information from third parties

Where appropriate and permitted by law, we may receive information from authentication providers, payment processors, analytics providers, business partners, and other services you choose to connect to our Services.

We will handle such information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and any applicable third-party terms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Providing services: To create accounts, operate features, process requests, and deliver purchased products.
  • Account management: To authenticate users, maintain profiles, and provide account support.
  • Payments: To process transactions, prevent payment fraud, and manage subscriptions.
  • Communication: To respond to inquiries, send service notices, and provide technical support.
  • Improvement: To understand usage patterns, troubleshoot problems, and improve performance and usability.
  • Security: To detect abuse, investigate suspicious activity, and protect our users and systems.
  • Marketing: To send promotional messages where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.
  • Legal compliance: To meet legal obligations, enforce our agreements, and respond to lawful requests.

We will use personal information only for purposes that are consistent with applicable law and this Policy.

We may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies to operate and improve our Services.
